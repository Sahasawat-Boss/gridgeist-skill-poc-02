02 — Process

Twelve weeks, six stages, fixed price.

Each stage ends with something you can read, argue with, or ship. If a stage fails its exit condition we stop and re-scope.

  1. Week 01

    Interview

    Ten buyer and team conversations, transcribed and coded.

  2. Week 02–03

    Position

    One statement, three rejected alternatives, and why.

  3. Week 04–05

    Scope

    Workstreams with effort and cost against each line.

  4. Week 06–08

    Design

    Flows and states reviewed in the browser, not in slides.

  5. Week 09–11

    Build

    Pull requests into your repository, your review rules.

  6. Week 12

    Hand over

    Docs, tokens and a recorded walkthrough for your team.
